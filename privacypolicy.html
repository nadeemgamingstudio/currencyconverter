Information Collection

ALL Currency Converter Live collects minimal information necessary for optimal functionality:

  • Conversion History: Recent currency conversions are stored locally on your device

All conversion data is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to external servers unless specifically required for app functionality.

Security Note: While we implement reasonable security measures, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure. We continuously work to protect your information to the best of our ability.
